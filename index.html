Game overview

What is Shift at Midnight?

A compact guide for the browser build: what the game is, how the core loop works, and what to watch for before the station turns hostile.

Shift at Midnight is a first-person detective horror and gas station management game where routine retail tasks become survival checks. Players stock shelves, process deliveries, question late-night customers, compare details, and decide who is human before a mistake turns the rest of the shift into a lockdown.

The pressure comes from doing ordinary work while your suspicion level keeps rising. You need enough rhythm to meet the nightly quota, but you also need enough patience to notice a false ID, a strange answer, or behavior that does not match the person in front of you.

Gameplay loop

How to play online

Think of every shift as a balance between service work and investigation. If the store falls apart, you lose control. If you trust the wrong customer, the night pushes back.

Work the station

Stock shelves, clean the store, take deliveries, serve customers, and keep your quota moving before sunrise.

Investigate customers

Ask questions, check IDs, compare details, and watch reactions. Small inconsistencies matter more than loud scares.

Lock down when wrong

If a threat gets through, barricade, set traps, hide, and choose your movement carefully. Panic wastes the few seconds you have.

Survival notes

Tips for surviving the night shift

Good play is methodical. Treat every strange detail as evidence, not just atmosphere.

Interrogate with purpose

Do not spam questions. Ask for details you can verify against an ID, behavior pattern, store task, or previous answer.

Watch the small things

Most danger begins quietly: a mismatch, a delayed reaction, or a routine customer who suddenly feels out of place.

Keep your route clean

Know where the doors, counters, hiding spots, and fallback paths are before the store becomes unsafe.

Shift gears fast

Once something gets through, stop optimizing the store and start optimizing survival. Secure, hide, move, and reset.

FAQ

Online FAQ

Short answers for searchers who want to know what the game is, why it loads slowly, and how to avoid the known browser issue.

Is this the official Steam version?

This page hosts a web-embedded build for quick browser play and a guide built around online play. The full PC game is listed separately on Steam.

What kind of game is it?

It is a detective-leaning horror simulation set inside a late-night gas station. You juggle store work with investigating customers who may be doppelgangers.

Why does the browser build take a while to load?

The WebGL build downloads several large Unity data and WebAssembly parts before the game starts. On a first visit, waiting around a minute is normal.

What should I avoid in the browser build?

Avoid picking up the flashlight on the desk. The reference page warns that it can freeze or soft-lock the browser build.

Can I play on mobile?

The page is responsive, but the game is best played with keyboard and mouse. A desktop browser gives the most reliable WebGL performance and controls.
